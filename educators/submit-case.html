The submission form

Click the button below to open our submission form. Please provide as much detail as you're comfortable sharing, including:

  • Your context: What subject do you teach? What level (undergraduate, postgraduate)?
  • The scenario: What happened? What was the AI integrity concern?
  • The values: Which of the values we discussed (honesty, transparency, independence, care, responsibility) came into tension?
  • What you tried: How did you respond? What worked, or what would you do differently?

Your submission will be reviewed by our research team. We may ask permission to include your case (anonymously) in future educator resources. You always retain the right to decline.
